# Code of conduct

This community works together on experimental Vanguard compatibility in
Proton. Contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds are welcome.
The standards here apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project spaces.

## Community standards

- Treat people with respect. Discuss technical disagreements using evidence,
  and distinguish criticism of an implementation from criticism of a person.
- Give useful context and be open to correction. Credit other people's work
  and state the limits of results honestly.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials, private
  messages, or sensitive security findings without permission.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct,
  deliberate disruption, and repeated unwanted contact.
- Do not pressure contributors to bypass vendor protections or claim support
  that the project has not demonstrated.

## Reporting and enforcement

The initial responsible maintainer is
[@erick-dsnk](https://github.com/erick-dsnk). For a public moderation concern,
raise an issue with only information suitable for public sharing. For a
sensitive concern, ask the maintainer to arrange a confidential contact
channel without posting the sensitive details. This repository does not yet
list a dedicated private conduct-reporting address.

The maintainer may clarify expectations, remove inappropriate content, ask a
participant to stop, restrict participation, or block a participant according
to the severity and persistence of the behavior. Reports are considered in
context. Share report details only as needed to handle the concern, and avoid
retaliation against people who report concerns in good faith. Participants may
ask the maintainer to reconsider an action with additional relevant context.
